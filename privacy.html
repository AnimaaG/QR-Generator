Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Local QR Generator ("we", "our", "the website", or "the service") is a browser-based QR code generation tool. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the website.

Privacy-first design: QR data entered into the generator is processed locally in your browser whenever supported by the application. The website does not operate a server that receives or stores the QR content you enter or the files referenced by your QR codes.

1. How the Website Works

Local QR Generator creates QR codes directly in your web browser. Text, links, and other supported QR data are processed locally on your device.

The website does not intentionally upload ordinary QR text, links, or QR contents to a server operated by us.

2. Large Files

A QR code cannot practically contain a large photo, video, PDF, application, document, or other large file. For large files, you may store the file yourself using Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, or another storage provider of your choice.

You can then provide a shareable URL to Local QR Generator. The website encodes that URL into the QR code. The website does not upload, download, copy, or store the referenced file.

3. File Sharing and External Services

You are responsible for the sharing permissions of any external file URL you provide. If you make a file publicly accessible, anyone who obtains the relevant link may be able to access the file according to the sharing rules of the storage provider.

Local QR Generator does not control external storage providers, external websites, or the availability and security of external URLs.

Before distributing a QR code, verify that the destination and sharing permissions are appropriate for your intended audience.

4. Information We Receive

The QR generator does not require an account, Google login, email address, or other personal information to generate QR codes.

We do not intentionally collect or store the QR data entered into the generator.

Like most websites, the hosting, network, browser, and internet infrastructure used to access the website may process ordinary technical information required to deliver web pages and services. Such information may include technical request information such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request timing.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

The website may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense or other advertising partners.

When advertisements are displayed, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device information, or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and improve advertising and to help prevent fraud or repeated display of the same advertisement.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Depending on applicable settings, consent requirements, and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized based on information associated with a user's browsing activity.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings .

Additional information about how Google uses data when advertising is available through Google's "How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps" .

Third-party advertising providers may have their own privacy policies and options for managing advertising preferences.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The QR generation functionality itself does not require cookies to process the QR data entered by users.

If advertising is enabled, advertising providers may place or read cookies and use similar technologies as part of their advertising and measurement systems.

Users can control cookies through their browser settings. Blocking or deleting cookies may affect certain website or advertising functionality.

7. Advertising Consent and Regional Requirements

Where applicable law or Google's advertising requirements require user consent for cookies, personalized advertising, or related processing, appropriate consent mechanisms may be presented to users.

For users in regions subject to specific advertising consent requirements, Google may use an approved consent management mechanism to obtain and manage the required choices.

Users may change available advertising privacy choices through the controls provided by Google or the applicable consent mechanism.

8. Browser Storage

The current application does not require browser storage to create or maintain a user account or cloud-storage connection.

The browser may retain normal cache, temporary files, cookies, or other browser-managed information according to the browser's own settings and any third-party services used on the website.

9. Third-Party Services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages and may load its QR-generation library from a public third-party CDN.

The website may also use third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. Those services operate according to their own policies, terms, technologies, and technical requirements.

The application does not use Google OAuth or the Google Drive API for storing user files.

10. QR Data and Personal Information

Users should avoid entering passwords, private keys, payment credentials, authentication tokens, confidential business information, or other highly sensitive information into a QR code unless they fully understand the security and sharing implications.

Local QR Generator does not intentionally create a personal profile from the QR data entered into the generator.

11. Security

Processing QR data locally reduces the need to transmit that data to an application server. However, no website, browser, network, or internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for verifying the destination of a QR code before sharing or distributing it.

12. Children's Privacy

The website is not specifically designed for children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

The website should not be used to intentionally collect or process information from children in violation of applicable laws or advertising requirements.

13. User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content you encode, the links you provide, and the sharing permissions of external files or URLs.

You are also responsible for ensuring that your use of the generated QR code complies with applicable laws and the terms of any external service linked through the QR code.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's functionality, advertising services, technologies, or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning Local QR Generator, please use the contact information made available by the website operator.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Local QR Generator ("we", "our", or "the website") provides a browser-based QR code generation service. This Privacy Policy explains how information and files are handled when you use the website.

Important: The website is designed to process ordinary QR information directly in your browser. Files that require external storage are uploaded to the Google Drive account that you explicitly authorize. We do not operate our own server for storing your uploaded files.

1. How the Website Works

Local QR Generator allows you to create QR codes from text, links, and other supported information. Where possible, QR data is processed directly inside your web browser.

The website does not intentionally upload ordinary text or link information to our own servers for QR generation.

2. File Storage

Some QR codes may contain a link to a file, such as an image, video, document, or PDF. Because large files cannot practically be stored inside a QR code itself, the website may need to upload the selected file to cloud storage.

When file storage is required, the website uses your Google Drive account after you explicitly authorize access. The application creates or uses the application-specific folder associated with Local QR Generator and uploads the selected file there.

Your files remain in your Google Drive account. We do not maintain a separate copy of your uploaded files on our own server.

3. Google Account Authorization

Google authorization is requested only when a feature requires Google Drive storage. You must explicitly authorize the application before it can perform the requested Google Drive operation.

The application requests the Google Drive permission required for the application's file operations. The current Google Drive scope used by the application is:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission is intended to allow the application to work with files created or opened through the application rather than providing unrestricted access to your entire Google Drive.

4. Google User Data

When you authorize Google Drive access, Google provides the application with authorization credentials that allow the requested Drive operation. These credentials are used by the browser application to perform the operation you requested.

The application does not intentionally collect your Google password. Your Google password is handled directly by Google.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not use Google Drive content for unrelated purposes.

5. Files You Upload

You are responsible for the files you choose to upload. Do not upload confidential, illegal, harmful, or sensitive material unless you have the appropriate rights and authorization to do so.

The application does not claim ownership of files that you upload. Files uploaded through the application remain associated with your Google Drive account.

6. QR Codes

A QR code containing ordinary text or a web link can be generated locally in your browser. For files, the QR code may contain a Google Drive file link rather than the actual file contents.

Anyone who can access a QR code containing a usable file link may potentially be able to access that link according to the sharing permissions configured for the corresponding Google Drive file. You are responsible for reviewing the sharing permissions of files you upload.

7. Data Stored Locally in Your Browser

The website may use browser storage such as localStorage to remember non-sensitive application preferences or information needed to improve your next visit.

Browser-stored information can be removed by clearing the website's browser data.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Local QR Generator does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers for QR generation. Browser functionality required by Google authentication may involve Google's own authentication mechanisms.

9. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services required to provide specific functionality, including:

  • Google Authentication and Google Drive API for authorized Drive operations.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting the website.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

10. Revoking Google Access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings. After revoking access, the application will no longer be able to use the previously granted authorization.

11. Deleting Uploaded Files

Because uploaded files are stored in your Google Drive account, you can manage or delete those files directly from Google Drive. Deleting the application or removing Google authorization does not necessarily delete files that were already uploaded to your Drive.

12. Security

The website uses HTTPS when served through GitHub Pages and uses Google's OAuth authorization mechanisms for Google Drive access.

However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security. You should use appropriate caution when uploading files and sharing QR codes.

13. Children's Privacy

The website is not specifically designed for children. We do not intentionally collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

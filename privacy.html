Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Local QR Generator ("we", "our", or "the website") is a browser-based QR code generation tool. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the website.

Core privacy principle: QR data is processed in your browser. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your entered QR data or uploaded files.

1. How the Website Works

Text, links and other supported QR data are processed locally in your browser and are used to generate the QR code on your device.

The website does not intentionally upload ordinary text, links or QR contents to a server operated by us.

2. Large Files

A QR code cannot practically contain a large photo, video, PDF or other large file. For large files, you are asked to store the file yourself using a cloud storage provider or another service of your choice and provide a shareable URL.

The website only reads the URL you paste into the page and places that URL into the QR code. The website does not upload, download, copy or store the referenced file.

3. File Sharing and Privacy

You are responsible for the sharing permissions of the file URL you provide. If you make a file publicly accessible, anyone who obtains the relevant link may be able to access the file according to the storage provider's sharing rules.

Before distributing a QR code, verify that the file's sharing settings are appropriate for your intended audience.

4. Information We Receive

The application is designed so that the QR data you enter stays in your browser. We do not require an account, email address, Google login, or other personal information to generate QR codes.

The website may receive ordinary technical information from its hosting or network infrastructure, such as information that is normally processed when a web page is requested. We do not use the QR generator itself to build a personal profile from your QR data.

5. Browser Storage

The current application does not require browser storage to remember a user account or cloud-storage connection. Your browser may retain normal web cache or other browser-managed data according to its own settings.

6. Third-Party Services

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The QR generation library is loaded from a public CDN. These third-party services may process technical request information according to their own policies.

The application does not use Google Drive API or Google OAuth for file storage.

7. Cookies and Tracking

Local QR Generator does not intentionally use advertising cookies or tracking systems to profile users.

8. Security

The website is designed to process QR data locally, which reduces the need to transmit that data to an application server. However, no internet service or browser environment can guarantee absolute security.

Do not enter passwords, private keys, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information into a QR code unless you understand the security and sharing implications.

9. User Responsibility

You are responsible for the content you encode, the links you provide, and the sharing permissions of any external file or URL.

10. Children's Privacy

The website is not specifically designed for children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's functionality or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the contact information provided by the website operator.

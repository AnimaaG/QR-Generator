How it works

1. Text, links and small information are processed locally in your browser.

2. Files such as photos, videos and PDFs are stored in your own Google Drive.

3. This website does not store your files on its own server.

4. Google Drive access is requested only when file storage is required.

5. The generated QR contains the information or your Google Drive file link.

6. You can download the generated QR as an image.

By continuing, you understand that files requiring storage will be uploaded to the Google Drive account that you authorize.

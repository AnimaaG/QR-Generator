Local QR Generator

Create QR codes directly in your browser without uploading your QR data to our servers.

How it works

1. Text, links and other small QR data are processed locally in your browser.

2. Large files cannot be stored inside a normal QR code.

3. For a large file, upload it to your own cloud storage and make its share link accessible to the people who need it.

4. Paste that shareable URL here and this website will generate a QR containing only that URL.

5. This website does not upload, store or manage your files.

6. You can download the generated QR as an image.

Do not paste private or confidential file links into a QR unless you understand the sharing permissions of the service hosting that file.
